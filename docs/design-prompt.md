Design a complete, premium UI for NEXUS, a personal digital-world platform. I need a full design system and every screen listed below, for desktop (1440px) and phone (390px).

WHAT NEXUS IS
NEXUS brings a person's whole life into one connected place: their people, their places, their plans and their growth. Instead of switching between a social app, a calendar, a to-do app, a learning app, a map and a marketplace, everything lives in one ecosystem where each part feeds the others. Tagline: "Your world. Your people. Your places. Your life."

WHO IT IS FOR
Ambitious young people aged 16 to 30, starting in Kigali, Rwanda: students, creators, young professionals and small business owners. They use phones first, often on shared or slow connections, and they want something that feels as polished as the best global apps while clearly belonging to their city.

THE FEELING
Premium consumer product, cinematic and alive, never a backend admin panel. Think Apple product pages, Linear's precision, Spotify's warmth and Airbnb's photography, combined. Confident, bold, calm. It should make someone say "this is a real startup" in the first three seconds.

VISUAL DIRECTION
- Dark-first ("Midnight & Signal") with a proper light mode as well.
- Dark palette base: ground #0B0D10, surface #14171C, raised surface #1A1E25, border #232830, text #F5F7FA, muted text #8A93A3.
- One signature accent, Signal Blue #3B82F6, used only for actions, links, active states, important numbers and AI moments. Never as decoration.
- Each module gets its own secondary tint so it has a personality inside the same system: People (warm coral), Events (amber), Nearby (green), Discover (violet), Learning (teal), AI (purple-to-blue glow).
- It must NOT become "everything black with blue buttons". Richness comes from large real photography with dark overlays, layered surfaces for depth, subtle glows and gradients only where they earn it, and bold type.
- Typography: a bold, characterful display face for big headlines (heavy, confident, tight tracking) paired with a clean modern sans for UI and body text (Geist or Inter family), plus a mono face for numbers, times and codes. A strict type scale, not random sizes.
- Photography of real Kigali life: young people, cafés, co-working spaces, the city at night, hills, events, markets.
- Rounded but not bubbly: 12 to 16px radius on cards, full pills for chips.
- Motion: a branded preloader, smooth page transitions, scroll-driven reveals on the landing page, satisfying micro-interactions (checking off a task, sending a message, RSVP-ing).
- No thin decorative lines or crosshair ticks. Background art must be clearly visible, not hidden behind heavy dark overlays.

DESIGN SYSTEM (deliver first)
Colour tokens for dark and light, type scale, spacing, radius, shadows and glows, icon style, and components: buttons (primary, secondary, ghost, destructive, with loading states), inputs, textareas, selects, chips and filters, tabs, cards (plain, interactive, image cards), avatars and avatar stacks, badges, progress bars and rings, stat tiles, toasts, modals, bottom sheets for phone, empty states, skeleton loaders, the notification bell with its popover, and a command palette (Ctrl+K).

SCREENS

1. Preloader: the NEXUS mark forming or pulsing, under two seconds.

2. Landing page (public, logged out): a cinematic full-bleed hero with the tagline and a "Create your world" button. Then scroll sections, each showing one part of the product with a real UI preview: your people, your circles, events near you, the map, your planner, learning and streaks, the marketplace and wallet, AI that plans your day. Then social proof, a closing call to action and the footer.

3. Sign in and Sign up: split screen with photography on one side and the form on the other. Also forgot password, reset password and a "check your email" confirmation.

4. Onboarding wizard (5 steps with a progress indicator): name and username, city (default Kigali), interests (tappable chips: tech, music, football, design, business, fitness, faith, food, art, film, reading, entrepreneurship), goals, and a final "your world is ready" moment previewing their personalised home.

5. App shell: a collapsible left sidebar on desktop, grouped as NEXUS (Home, Discover, People, Feed, Messages), YOUR LIFE (Calendar, Tasks, Goals, Learn, Progress) and YOUR WORLD (Circles, Events, Nearby, Market, Wallet). A top bar with global search, theme toggle, notification bell and avatar menu. On phone, a bottom tab bar (Home, Discover, People, Feed, More).

6. Home: a greeting ("Good evening, Malvyn."), a "Needs you" alert card (for example "3 tasks are overdue"), Today's timeline (fixed commitments and free gaps), Next up tasks, Coming up events, a Nexus AI card ("You have 3h free from 14:00. Enough for a focused session on Java."), Goals with progress rings, a streak counter and friends' recent activity.

7. Discover: a personalised "For you" feed mixing events, places, circles, people and marketplace listings as big image cards. Every card shows a one-line reason ("Because you saved Question Coffee", "3 friends are going"). Category filters along the top.

8. People: suggested people with their reason ("12 mutual friends", "Also into design"), friends and friend requests, and the following and followers lists. Public profile page: cover image, avatar, bio, city, interests, stats, posts, and circles they belong to, with Follow, Add friend and Message buttons.

9. Feed: a post composer (text, photo, poll; visibility Public, Friends or Private), and posts with reactions, threaded comments and save. Poll results as animated bars.

10. Messages and Circles: circle directory (public, private, invite-only), circle page with cover, members and roles, posts and a chat tab. The chat should feel like WhatsApp or iMessage done beautifully: bubbles, reactions, replies, images, typing indicator, read receipts and online presence.

11. Events: event list and calendar, and an event detail page with cover image, host, time, place with a mini map, capacity bar ("12 spots left"), attendee avatars, RSVP (Going or Interested), invite friends, and a host-only check-in code screen.

12. Nearby: a large interactive dark map of Kigali with category-coloured pins and cluster bubbles, a place list beside it (a bottom sheet on phone), category chips (Cafés, Food, Workspaces, Venues, Stores, Sport, Services), a "Use my location" button with distances, and a place detail page with photos, rating, reviews, save and upcoming events there.

13. Planner: a week calendar view merging fixed blocks, events and due tasks, with free gaps highlighted. A task list with priority, due dates, recurrence and goal links. Goals with milestones and derived progress.

14. Plan my day (AI): a timeline of today with proposed blocks placed into free time, each with a one-sentence reason from the AI, and Accept, Decline and "Accept all" buttons. Make the AI moments feel special (subtle glow, spark icon) without being gimmicky.

15. Learn and Progress: learning paths made of modules, a study timer and a logged-time view. The Progress page has a big streak number, a twelve-week activity heatmap, and stats for tasks, milestones, modules, study minutes and event check-ins.

16. Market, Orders and Wallet: the marketplace grid of listings from local businesses, a listing detail page, and a business page with its owner, manager and staff team. An order flow where the seller accepts before payment. Wallet: balance in RWF (no decimals), send money, top-up and a transaction history.

17. Notifications: a centre grouped by today and earlier, plus per-category notification settings.

18. Settings: profile, account, privacy (including a personalisation opt-out), notifications, theme and sessions.

19. Admin (for moderators): a moderation queue of reports showing the reported content, user suspensions and an analytics dashboard.

STATES TO SHOW
Empty states with helpful illustrations and one clear action, loading skeletons, error states, and success toasts.
